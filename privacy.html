Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Operator and contact

MOWONI is operated by Discord user @hyelitaa. Contact @hyelitaa on Discord for privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests. This policy covers the MOWONI bot. Discord and any hosting provider process information under their own policies.

2. Information processed

MOWONI processes Discord user and server identifiers and interaction information needed to handle commands and buttons. Usernames, mentions and avatars may be used to display Discord profiles or responses. Discord role information is checked to restrict staff commands. Account creation time can be derived from a Discord user ID to identify recent accounts for staff review.

Stored player data includes card inventories and quantities; cookies and sweets; binders and folders and their names; wishlists; optional profile bios, favourite cards, bias and stan lists; daily streaks and cooldown timestamps; achievements, quests, ingredients, mini-game progress and virtual companion choices, nicknames and care progress. Older saved decoration data may remain after retirement of the bakery decoration feature.

Transaction and audit records may include the players involved, server ID where recorded, transaction IDs, timestamps, card IDs, quantities, currency amounts, sweets and snapshots of card descriptions. These records cover gifts, trades, purchases, sales, pulls, claims, staff grants, catalogue changes and other supported game actions. Staff may supply reasons for administrative actions. Catalogue image files and metadata submitted through staff commands are also stored.

The bot does not require users to provide passwords, authentication codes, payment details, identity documents or their private address. Its current implementation uses slash-command interactions and does not read or retain ordinary chat message content or direct-message conversations. Text deliberately submitted through a bot command, modal or profile field is different and may be stored as described above. Do not put sensitive personal information in these fields.

3. Purposes

Information is used to provide gameplay, save collections and progress, calculate rewards and cooldowns, carry out confirmed exchanges, display requested profiles and collections, provide support and investigate abuse. In-game cookies are virtual currency, not browser tracking cookies. The current bot code does not include advertising trackers or a data-sale feature.

4. Public information and access

Bot messages posted to a server can be seen by people who have access to that channel. Supported commands let other users view player profiles, card collections, folders, binders and companions. Treat profile text and game collection information as visible to other players. Users can edit or clear supported profile fields using the relevant commands.

Staff-only logs are restricted to the configured staff role. The dedicated /logs commands show records for the current server and are delivered privately to the requesting staff member. Logs include gifts and selected activity flagged for review, such as pulls involving recent Discord accounts or repeated gifts. A flag is not proof of alternate-account abuse and does not automatically impose a sanction.

The operator and authorized people administering the bot's storage can access saved records for operation, support and security. Discord delivers interactions, messages and attachments. Data is stored in files on the computer or server running the bot; hosting infrastructure administrators may have access as part of providing that service. Actual hosting arrangements can change and can be requested from the operator. Information is not made publicly accessible simply because it is stored, except through the public bot features described above.

5. Retention

Player progress is kept between sessions so the game can continue. The current version does not automatically delete an entire player record after inactivity. Many transaction and staff audit records also have no automatic expiration in the current code. Some temporary interaction state expires and some game records are cleaned up separately. Removing the bot from a server does not itself erase all player data or transaction history. Data may also appear in backups created by the operator or hosting service.

The operator should remove or anonymize information when it is no longer necessary. To request deletion without waiting for an inactivity cleanup, use the contact procedure below. Any continued retention of limited information for a specific unresolved security, legal or transaction issue should be explained to the requester, including its reason and relevant period, rather than treated as blanket permission to retain all data indefinitely.

6. Your requests

Contact Discord @hyelitaa with your Discord user ID and the request: for example, "Please delete my MOWONI player data." Access, correction and deletion requests are handled manually; the current bot does not provide a complete automatic account-deletion command. The operator may verify control of the relevant Discord account, but will not need its password, authentication codes or a government identity document.

Deleting player data removes associated saved game progress. Requests should also be reviewed for identifying references in transaction and staff records, rather than deleting only the inventory entry. The operator should explain the outcome and any justified exceptions. Copies of already posted Discord messages or data held by other players are not automatically removed by deleting a player record. Backup handling and any applicable retention limits should be explained when processing a request.

7. Security and policy updates

Access to saved information should be limited to people who need it to run and support MOWONI. No online service can promise perfect security. Report suspected data exposure to @hyelitaa. The update date identifies this policy version; changes in data practices should be reflected in the published policy and communicated where appropriate.
